Survival Level 100 Uploader

Survival Level 100 Uploader is a small personal desktop tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Survival Level 100 (animated survival stories) to upload and schedule the channel's own Shorts.

It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and is used only with the channel owner's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's finished videos via the official YouTube Data API v3 (videos.insert).
  • Sets the title, description, tags, category, "not made for kids" flag and the scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set) when available.

It does not read, analyze or store data about other YouTube users or channels.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in once with Google OAuth (consent screen, scopes youtube.upload and youtube).
  2. The token is saved locally on the owner's PC (token.json).
  3. The owner adds finished videos to a local queue (yt_queue.json).
  4. The app uploads each video with videos.insert as private with a scheduled publish time (one per day) and sets the thumbnail.
  5. YouTube publishes the video at the scheduled time.
