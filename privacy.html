Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how Survival Level 100 Uploader ("the app"), a personal desktop tool for uploading videos to the YouTube channel Survival Level 100, handles data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth 2.0 access/refresh token for the signed-in YouTube account, granted by the user through Google's consent screen (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube).
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and schedule times that the user prepares for upload.
  • The IDs of the videos it uploads, so it can track which videos are done.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and schedule the user's own videos on the user's own channel.
  • The OAuth token and the upload queue are stored only locally on the user's computer (token.json, yt_queue.json). The app has no server; nothing is sent anywhere except to Google/YouTube APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used.

Deleting data and revoking access

Delete token.json from the app folder to remove the stored token. You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
